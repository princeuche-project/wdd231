Healthy Eating and Nutrition Site Plan

Site Name

Healthy Life Nutrition

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to educate visitors about healthy eating habits, balanced diets, nutrition, meal planning, and healthy lifestyle choices. The website will provide practical tips and easy-to-understand information that helps people improve their overall health and well-being.

Target Audience

The target audience includes:

  • Teenagers and young adults
  • Individuals interested in improving their eating habits
  • Beginners learning about nutrition
  • Parents and families

Website Goals

  • Help users understand the basics of nutrition
  • Encourage healthier eating habits
  • Provide meal planning ideas
  • Explain essential nutrients and their benefits
  • Share practical lifestyle and wellness tips

Site Map

  • Welcome message
  • Introduction to healthy eating
  • Benefits of proper nutrition
  • Call-to-action buttons such as:
  • Learn More

Feature:

  • Hero image/banner
  • Motivational quote about health
  • Quick navigation cards

Nutrition Basics Page

  • Content:
  • What is nutrition?
  • Importance of vitamins and minerals
  • Daily nutritional needs

Healthy Meal Plans Page

Content:

  • Breakfast ideas
  • Lunch ideas
  • Dinner ideas
  • Weekly meal plans

Typography

  • Heading Font
  • Roboto, sans-serif
  • Body Font:
  • Arial or Open Sans

Responsive Design Plan

Mobile View

  • Hamburger navigation menu
  • Single-column layout
  • Desktop View
  • Multi-column layout
  • Horizontal navigation menu
  • Larger images and wider spacing

Reason for Choosing This Topic

I chose healthy eating and nutrition because proper nutrition plays an important role in maintaining good health and preventing diseases. Many people want to improve their eating habits but may not know where to begin. This website will provide useful and simple information that can help people make healthier food choices and improve their lifestyles.
